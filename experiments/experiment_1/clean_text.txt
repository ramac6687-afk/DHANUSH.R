
EXPERIMENT 1: AMPLITUDE MODULATION AND DEMODULATION

SOURCE:
Communication Systems-I Lab Manual
Pages: 1, 2 and 3

AIM:
To conduct an experiment to generate the AM signal and to demodulate the AM signal.

APPARATUS REQUIRED:
1. IC XR2206
2. Resistors
3. Capacitors
4. Diode OA79
5. Adapters

CIRCUIT:
The AM generation and demodulation circuit is provided in the original
lab manual on page 1. The original page image must be retained because
the circuit diagram contains visual connection information.

DESIGN / SPECIFICATIONS:
Carrier frequency:
fc = 100 KHz

The manual provides the design relation and component selection for
the AM circuit on page 1.

PROCEDURE:
1. Make the connections as shown in the circuit diagram.
2. With m(t)=0, apply DC 12V (Vc + Vcc).
3. Observe the unmodulated carrier at pin no. 2 (point A).
4. Apply a sinusoidal message signal with minimum amplitude.
5. Adjust the DC offset of the ASG so that the negative peak of the
   message is approximately zero.
6. Observe the amplitude modulated output at pin no. 2 (point A).
7. Measure Vmax and Vmin from the AM signal and find the modulation
   index μ. Also measure Vmax and Vmin of the carrier signal.
8. Vary the amplitude of the modulating signal.
9. Plot the graph of %μ/Vm versus Vm.
10. Connect the envelope detector circuit and observe the demodulated
    signal at point B. Compare it with m(t).

MODULATION INDEX:
The manual uses Vmax and Vmin of the AM waveform to determine the
modulation index.

The relationship is represented in the observation table on page 1.
The original table should be consulted for measured experimental values.

WAVEFORMS:
The manual shows:
1. Message signal m(t) with DC shift
2. Carrier signal c(t)
3. AM wave
4. Demodulated wave

The AM waveform envelope follows the message signal. The Vmax and Vmin
points are marked on the AM waveform.

DEMODULATION:
The AM signal is passed through an envelope detector.
The demodulated output is observed at point B and compared with the
original message signal m(t).

TRANSFER CHARACTERISTIC:
The manual includes a transfer characteristic curve showing Vmax and
Vmin and a demodulation observation table on page 3.
